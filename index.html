Eric Bonnet

Welcome to my home page. I'm a computational biologist working at the CEA and more precisely at the Centre National de Recherche en Génomique Humaine (CNRGH, National Center for Human Genomic Research). The CNRGH is one of the largest sequencing centres in Europe.

I am leading the computational genomics laboratory. We focus on the computational analysis of high-throughput genomic data related to human health and disease.
